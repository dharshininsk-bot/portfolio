01 Award Winner · Geek N Jam

Shakey

Genre: Strategy / AI-driven gameplay

Shakey is a game built around a reversal of roles: players first train a robot using basic movements and jumps, then face it in an inverted challenge where they must outsmart the AI.

The experience combines pathfinding, hazard detection, and deceptive level design to challenge how players think about intelligence and predictability.

My Contributions

  • Gameplay programming and AI behaviour.
  • Game mechanics and level design.
  • Asset creation and integration.
  • Implementing robot navigation and hazard-aware decision-making.

02 Experimental Game Concept

Home Sweet Home

Genre: Psychological Horror / Sensory Exploration

Home Sweet Home explores how self-doubt can narrow our perception of ourselves and the world around us. As the player's vision gradually deteriorates, a symbolic monster becomes an increasingly threatening presence.

With sight no longer reliable, players must escape using sound and their ability to interpret the environment through other senses. The monster represents how self-doubt distorts perception, making our world feel smaller and our fears appear larger.

Core Experience

  • Gradual loss of vision mechanic.
  • Sound-based environmental awareness and navigation.
  • A symbolic monster representing self-doubt.
  • Survival through sensory adaptation rather than conventional combat.

03 Remote Internship · Fusion VR

Interactive VR: Light & Dark Rooms

Focus: Virtual Reality · Interactive Learning · Custom Shader Dev

During my remote internship with Fusion VR, I worked under guidance on an interactive VR experience exploring additive and subtractive colour mixing.

The experience uses two contrasting environments—a light room and a dark room—to help players explore how colour behaves through interaction. A friendly bot accompanies players, guiding them through the experience.

My Contributions

  • Designed and built interactive light and dark rooms that serve as a sandboxed environment for real-time additive and subtractive colour mixing.
  • Developed custom optical shaders applied to lenses to accurately demonstrate subtractive colour mixing and light filtration through glass.
  • Created an intuitive, guided VR learning experience with real-time visual feedback and companion bot navigation.
  • Technical Focus: Custom shader development, optical physics simulation, and translating visual colour theory into interactive gameplay mechanics.
